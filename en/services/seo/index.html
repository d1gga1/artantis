SEO & search visibility

Get found by people already looking for you.

There is a vast difference between interrupting someone with an advert and appearing at the exact moment they are searching for what you sell. SEO works on the second case: no intrusion, just the right answer at the right time. It takes patience, but traffic that arrives this way does not stop when you stop paying.

What we work on

SEO is not a switch. It is work across four fronts that move together: look after only one and the other three cancel it out.

01

Technical audit

How Google actually sees your site: speed, URL structure, structured data, duplicate pages, crawl errors. This is often where the problems blocking everything else are hiding.

02

Keyword research

Which words your customers really use, at what volume and against how much competition. It replaces guessing with knowing where you have a real chance of ranking.

03

Content and structure

One page for each thing you sell, written to answer one precise question. Generic pages that talk about everything rank for nothing.

04

Local SEO

Google Business Profile, consistency of name, address and phone number, reviews. For anyone working in a specific area it is by far the biggest source of enquiries.

What we can promise and what we cannot

Our industry is full of promises that do not hold up: "number one on Google in 30 days", "guaranteed position". Nobody can guarantee that, because nobody controls Google’s algorithm. Anyone promising it either does not know, or is counting on you not knowing.

What we can guarantee is method and transparency: you know what we are doing, why, and which numbers we are watching. Results do come, but on a scale of months rather than weeks, and the curve is slow at first and steep later.

  • Realistic timelines. First movement shows in 2-3 months, solid results between month six and twelve. A brand new domain starts further back than one that has existed for years.
  • Reports you can understand. Every month: which searches bring you visits, how positions are moving, what we did and what comes next. In plain language, not jargon.
  • No shortcuts. No bought links, no bulk-generated text. Shortcuts work until Google notices, and then the bill arrives all at once.
  • Your data, your logins. Search Console and analytics are in your name. If you change provider one day, the history stays with you.

How the arrangement works

SEO is not a job you deliver once. It is a continuous process, which is why it is usually structured as a monthly collaboration rather than a closed project.

It almost always starts with an initial audit, which is worth having on its own: it tells you where you stand and what needs fixing, and it stays yours even if you then decide to proceed alone or with someone else.

From there, if continuing makes sense, we agree a monthly commitment sized to the industry and the competition. The quote is fixed price like all our work, and any minimum duration is stated up front, not afterwards.

Frequently asked questions

The answers that actually matter.

How long until results show?

First movement in 2-3 months, solid results between month six and twelve. It depends a lot on how competitive the industry is and how old the domain is. Anyone promising page one in a month is selling you something else.

Do you guarantee first position on Google?

No, and be wary of anyone who does: nobody controls Google’s algorithm. What we guarantee is the method, transparency on the numbers, and that every euro spent is traceable to work actually done.

Is it useful if my site was built by someone else?

That is often the best case. We analyse what is there, what is worth keeping and what is holding it back. Rebuilding everything is not always the smartest or the cheapest choice.

Does SEO replace paid campaigns?

They do different jobs. Campaigns bring enquiries immediately but stop when you stop paying; SEO takes months but then keeps working. In most cases it pays to run both: campaigns cover the wait while SEO grows.
