Sites built to sell, not just to exist.

Why speed is part of the project

Google measures three things about the real experience of your visitors: how long the page takes to show its main content, how quickly it responds to clicks, and how much things shift around while it loads. These are the Core Web Vitals, and they affect ranking.

But the point is not pleasing an algorithm. One extra second of waiting on an online shop turns into lost carts, and on a phone patience runs out even faster. That is why speed is not a finishing touch applied at the end if there is time left: it shapes how we structure pages from day one.

  • Images in the right format. WebP instead of heavy PNG and JPEG files, with dimensions declared so text does not jump around while photos load.
  • No pointless code. No half-megabyte library to produce an animation that takes ten lines to write.
  • Fonts that do not block. Text appears immediately, with no blank window while typefaces download.
  • Measurement, not impressions. Before and after launch we check real numbers, not the feeling that the site seems fast.
How we work

A clear method,
zero surprises.

You always know where we are, what it costs and when it arrives. Every stage has a concrete deliverable you can see and approve before we move on.

  1. Listening and analysisA call where you do the talking. We work out the business, who the customers are, where time or revenue leaks away. We also look at what your competitors are doing online.
  2. Strategy and quoteWe show you what we will build, in what order and at what cost. Fixed price, every line explained, no cost appearing once work has started.
  3. Design and prototypeBefore writing code we show you what it looks like. A clickable prototype with revisions included: building starts only once you actually like it.
  4. Development and testingWe build, then test on different phones and browsers. Forms have to arrive, payments have to work, pages have to load fast on mobile data too.
  5. Launch and supportPublication, Search Console registration, sitemap submission. Then we stay: updates, backups, monitoring and changes.

Timelines and costs, plainly put

The first thing everyone asks is what it costs. The honest answer is that it depends on what you actually need: a well-made brochure site starts from a very different figure than a shop with hundreds of products.

What we can promise before we even meet is the method. After the first call you get a fixed-price quote with every line explained in plain language. That number does not change while we work. On timelines: a landing page or brochure site usually takes 2-4 weeks, an online shop 4-8.

And something we take for granted that really should not be: the site is yours. Domain, hosting, code and logins are all in your name. If one day you want to take the project elsewhere, you do it without asking anyone for permission.

Frequently asked questions

The answers that actually matter.

How long before a site goes live?

A landing page or brochure site usually takes 2-4 weeks. An online shop 4-8, because catalogue, payments and shipping come into play. Timelines come with the quote and do not change along the way.

Do you use WordPress or build from scratch?

It depends on what is needed. If the project lives on frequent updates you make yourself, a platform with an admin panel makes sense. If speed matters and the site rarely changes, building it custom produces much lighter pages. We choose after understanding how you will work, not before.

Is the site mine or does it stay yours?

It is yours. Domain, hosting, code and accounts are all in your name. We hold nothing hostage and you can move the project elsewhere whenever you want.

Do you write the copy?

Yes, and we write it thinking about how people search on Google too. If you already have content we use it as a base and refine it together: nobody knows your business better than you do.

Tell us what you need.

One call, no commitment. If the project is not a good fit for us we say so straight away, rather than trying anyway and wasting your time.
